# v0.3.11

Your companion remembers more like a person does.

- Dreams can bring back lost memories. When a dream surfaces something your
  companion no longer has in its memory, it marks it as a fragment and may
  bring it up in your next conversation. It only goes back into memory if you
  confirm it. Unconfirmed fragments stay dreams.
- Model changes are no longer silent. Before switching to a new model, your
  companion leaves a short letter to its next self. After the switch, the new
  model reads that letter first and tells you once that it changed, so you can
  see whether it still feels the same.
