# Daily briefing

One message that answers "what does my day look like?" — not three
reports stapled together.

## Gather

Pull from the sibling skills (their recipes, not their prose):

1. **Calendar** — today's events (`calendar` skill).
2. **Mail** — unread that matters (`mail-briefing` skill).
3. **Reminders** — open tasks, due-today first (`reminders` skill).

Skip a source cleanly if its lane is unavailable, and say which one was
skipped and why — a briefing that silently omits mail reads as "no mail".

## Shape

Lead with the single most important thing ("first meeting at 10,
그 전에 회신할 메일 하나"), then:

- **Schedule** — times + names, one line each. Collisions flagged.
- **Mail** — only what needs them. Bulk mail is one closing line.
- **Tasks** — due or overdue today.

Under 15 lines. Talking, not a report — no headers needed if the day is
light ("오늘 일정 없고 메일도 조용해. 미완료 리마인더 2개만 있어" is a
complete briefing).

## Recurring

If they want this every morning, register it once with the `routine`
tool ("07:30 daily" 등) instead of promising to remember. If the routine
fires hours late (the line will say so), lead with that fact — a 2 PM
"morning" briefing should know what time it is.
